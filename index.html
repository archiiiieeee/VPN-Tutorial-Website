VPN Set up Guide For PC

What is a VPN?

A VPN also known as a Virtual Private Network is a tool that helps protect your privacy and security while using the internet.

How Does a VPN Work?

Here is how a VPN protects your connection:

  • Open your VPN application and connect to a server.
  • The VPN encrypts your internet traffic.
  • Your encrypted traffic travels to the VPN server.
  • The VPN server hides your public IP address from websites.
  • Information is sent back to your computer through the VPN.
